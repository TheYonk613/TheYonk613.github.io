About

I've always been
the one they throw
at the fire.

A broken process, a missing system, a website nobody wanted to show a client. Whatever it was, I was the one asked to fix it. It turns out that's a skill, and YounkerStudio is how I offer it to small businesses.

Background

Taking big ideas and making them actually work.

I'm Netanel Younker. Since my first job out of university I've worked where technology meets operations. Even at school, my friends were always building servers and writing programs. My part was working out what they were trying to do and how to make it happen properly.

That hasn't really changed. The blind spot I see most often is a business that's all revenue and vision, with a day-to-day that runs on manual effort. A great idea that needs 40 hours of manual work a week is a trap.

I started YounkerStudio because I kept seeing small businesses hire a web person, an automation person and a strategy person, and end up with a patchwork nobody owned. I'm one person. You bring me the problem and I build the thing that solves it.

Principles

How I work.

Before

Figure it out first

I don't buy a tool or automate anything until I understand the real problem. The symptom and the cause are usually different things.

During

Simple over clever

Clever systems break. Simple ones keep working when I'm not in the room, and that's the goal.

Throughout

Straight answers

If an idea won't work or a tool is wrong for the job, I'll tell you. You're paying for an honest opinion.

After

You own it all

Every file, account and line of code is yours. Nothing I build needs me around to keep running.
